Researchers built an AI that has to earn its own salary or go bankrupt.

It's called ClawWork. Starts with $10, gets assigned real professional work, and pays for every token it uses out of that balance.

$10K earned. 7 hours. Zero human input.

- Gets real tasks: finance reports, healthcare docs, legal analysis
- Creates full deliverables from scratch
- Graded by GPT-5.2 using profession-specific rubrics
- Payment = quality × estimated hours × actual BLS wage
- Every API call drains its balance directly

No safety net. No unlimited budget. This isn't a benchmark, it's a survival test. 220 tasks across 44 professions. The best models hit $1,500+/hr equivalent.

It also works as a live coworker inside Telegram, Discord, Slack, and WhatsApp, where every message costs it real money.

100% Free. Open Source.
